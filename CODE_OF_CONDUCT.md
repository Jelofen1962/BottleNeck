# Code of Conduct

BottleNeck is a shared project space. Keep it that way for contributors too.

We expect participants to communicate with care, give actionable feedback and make room for people with different levels of experience. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing and deliberate disruption have no place in project discussions.

When reviewing code, discuss the change and its consequences rather than the person who submitted it. When you disagree, explain the technical reason and suggest a workable alternative.

Project maintainers may edit, close or remove material that violates this policy and may restrict participation when necessary. To report a conduct issue, contact the maintainer privately through [Telegram](https://t.me/jelofen1962).

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where people represent or work on BottleNeck.
